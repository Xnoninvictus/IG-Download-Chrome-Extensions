IG Saved Posts Exporter

To export your saved posts:

  1. Go to instagram.com/your_username/saved/
  2. Wait for the page to load
  3. Click the 📦 Select All & Export button (top-right corner)
  4. The extension scrolls through all saved posts, collecting URLs
  5. A .txt file downloads automatically with all post/reel links
💡 The button is semi-transparent by default. Hover over it to make it fully visible.
